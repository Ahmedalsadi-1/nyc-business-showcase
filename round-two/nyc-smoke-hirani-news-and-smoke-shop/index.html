THE DESIGN

01

A newsstand name
deserves a masthead.

This independent proposal starts with Hirani's listed name and address on Broadway in Astoria. Newspaper proportions offer a natural visual reference for a business listed as a news and smoke shop.

A generous masthead leads into a focused reading layout. Rather than fabricate photographs, this preview uses an original type composition that makes the street number part of the identity.

The artwork is a proposal, not a real publication or shop sign. Current opening hours, photographs and final business wording would be confirmed with the owner before launch.
