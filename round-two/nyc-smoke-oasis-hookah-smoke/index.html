THE IDEA

A sense
of place,
drawn simply.

An arch, a horizon and the Avenue M address.

This independent website proposal for Oasis Hookah Smoke takes its visual starting point from the name. The doorway and layered landscape are original abstract artwork, not a photograph or a claim about the shop interior.

The public research record places the business at 1926 Avenue M in Brooklyn. Clear lettering and warm architectural colors put that location at the center of the concept.
