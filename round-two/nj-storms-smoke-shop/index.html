A website direction drawn from Storms Avenue itself.

The research record places Storms Smoke Shop at 124 Storms Avenue in Jersey City. This proposed identity draws a simple connection between the trading name and the street: architectural shapes, slate tones and deliberate diagonal strokes.

The existing web presence needs owner verification, including the preferred business name. Nothing in this preview replaces the current site or confirms trading hours.

What is artwork, and what is fact?

The illustrated buildings and rain lines are original abstract concept artwork. The listed address comes from the reviewed prospect record. Actual premises, operating details and final branding remain for the owner to approve.
