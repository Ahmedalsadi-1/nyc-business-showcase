01 / A place in Queens

Built around
an address.

Empire Smoke Shop is listed at 30-07 39th Avenue in Long Island City. This concept uses the weight and balance of architectural lettering to distinguish this particular address from businesses with similar names.

The letter becomes the landmark.

The sculptural E is original concept artwork. It does not represent the actual storefront, interiors or merchandise. This second-round design remains separate from the active campaign.
