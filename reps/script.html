Discuss changes and price

“This is a proposed website for your business. What would you change so it feels right to you? We would confirm your photos, wording, hours and any prices before launch.”

Open this business’s matching proposal for the current packages and scope. Agree on the actual features, domain, hosting and maintenance before discussing payment or a launch date.

Ask for the next step

“Would you like to go through the proposal together, or set a time when we can review your changes?”

If the owner is busy: “What day and time would be better for a quick look?” Record that follow-up on your route.
